Masroufy privacy policy

Last updated: 6 November 2026

This policy explains how Masroufy, the expense tracker for Android (com.marouanebatrone.masroufy on Google Play), handles your data. Masroufy is built so that your money data stays on your phone.

In short

  • No account and no servers: the developer never receives your data.
  • Everything you enter stays on your phone.
  • No ads, no trackers, no analytics.
  • This version sends nothing anywhere.

What Masroufy stores, and where

Everything you enter (the name you want to be called, income, savings target, expenses, fixed costs, goals, categories and settings) is stored in the app’s own storage on your phone only.

  • Masroufy has no account and no servers. The developer never receives your data, and neither does anyone else.
  • The app is excluded from Android’s cloud backup (Google Drive), so your data is not copied there either.
  • There are no ads, no trackers, no analytics and no crash reporting.
  • The app works without an internet connection.
  • This version of Masroufy never sends your data anywhere.

How your data is used

Only on your phone, to make the app work: what you can spend today, your history, insights, plan and goals, and the daily reminder if you turn it on. Your data is never sold, shared or used for advertising.

Sharing with third parties

None. Masroufy shares no data with anyone and contains no third-party code that collects data. Google Play, which distributes the app, handles downloads and updates under Google’s own privacy policy.

How your data is protected

  • Your data is kept in Masroufy’s private storage, which Android keeps out of reach of other apps.
  • You can lock the app with your fingerprint, face or PIN (Settings › Security). With the lock on, the app is also hidden in recent apps and screenshots are blocked.
  • Masroufy never sees your fingerprint or face: Android checks them and only tells the app whether it was you.

Keeping and deleting your data

Your data stays on your phone for as long as you keep it there. You see, change and delete all of it yourself, in the app, at any time.

  • Settings › Privacy › “Delete all my data” deletes everything from your phone and starts the setup again.
  • Uninstalling the app also deletes all its data.
  • As the developer holds no copy, there is nothing else to delete.

Files you export

A CSV export or a backup file is created only when you ask for it, and goes only where you choose to send or save it. Backups contain your data: keep them somewhere safe.

Permissions

Permission Used for
Internet Not used in this version (kept for Masroufy AI)
Notifications Only for the daily reminder, if you turn it on
Biometrics (fingerprint, face) Only for the app lock, if you turn it on

Android also lists a few technical permissions that give no access to your data: vibration (for the light taps), running at startup and wake lock (to keep the reminder planned), network state, and screen capture detection (required by the library that hides the app when it is locked; Masroufy doesn’t watch your screenshots).

Coming later: Masroufy AI (optional)

Masroufy AI, an assistant that analyzes your spending and suggests tips, is not part of this version. When it arrives, this policy will be updated before the release, and it will work as follows.

It will be off by default. If you turn it on and give your own OpenRouter key, each time you tap “Get my plan” the app sends a summary of your month to the AI model you chose, through OpenRouter:

  • totals, your plan (income, savings target, fixed costs), spending per category, the labels you typed (for example “taxi”), your goals and the previous advice;
  • never your name (including the name you asked Masroufy to call you), contacts, location, phone identifiers or anything else from your phone.

This request goes from your phone directly to OpenRouter with your key; the developer does not see it. OpenRouter and the model provider handle it under their own terms and privacy policies, which you accept when you create your OpenRouter account. Your key is kept in the phone’s secure storage (Android Keystore), and the reports are stored on your phone. You can turn the coach off and remove the key at any time.

Children

Masroufy is made for adults managing their own money. It is not directed at children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date. Changes that affect your data, such as Masroufy AI, will be published here before the version of the app that brings them.
